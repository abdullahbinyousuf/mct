ABOUT THE MCT DEPARTMENT

The Department of Multimedia and Creative Technology (MCT) at Daffodil International University is dedicated to nurturing creative professionals equipped with strong technical skills and artistic vision. The department focuses on the integration of design, media, and technology to meet the evolving demands of the digital industry.

OUR ACADEMIC FOCUS

The MCT Department offers a balanced curriculum combining creative design and technical expertise to prepare students for careers in multimedia, animation, UI UX, web technologies, and digital media.

Multimedia Design

Visual communication through graphics, animation, and interactive media.

Web Technology

Design and development of modern, responsive web applications.

Creative Media

Digital storytelling, video production, and creative content development.

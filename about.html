About MCT

The Department of Multimedia & Creative Technology (MCT) at Daffodil International University (DIU) is a pioneering academic unit dedicated to shaping the next generation of creative technologists.

info Who We Are

Leading Multimedia Education

As one of the foremost multimedia programs in Bangladesh, MCT blends artistic thinking with practical technological skills. We prepare students for careers in digital media, 3D design, animation, game development, film & media production, and emerging creative industries.

Our department nurtures imagination, creativity, and technical fluency, encouraging students to solve real-world problems and contribute meaningfully to the global digital landscape.
